# Music discovery

Scope: Metrolist v14 on Android 7+, iOS 17+, Windows 10+, macOS 15+ and Linux unless stated otherwise. The KMP rewrite is now Metrolist in the public MetrolistGroup/Metrolist repository. Menu details below come from pre-v14 source review, not v14 device tests, and may differ. Ask for platform/version and the visible label if needed.

Use for Home recommendations and Your daily discover, separate from account-playlist sync.

Historical standalone Android 13.x had Your daily discover on Home, based on up to five liked songs and their related recommendations. It was a recommendation section, not a saved Library playlist. An active Home filter or no recommendations could hide it. This behavior was reviewed at the public v13.7.0 tag in HomeScreen.kt, HomeViewModel.kt and your_daily_discover resources. These 13.x labels/conditions may differ in v14 on every platform.

Do not deny discovery because there is no saved playlist with that exact name. Do not invent a Discover tab, Browse menu, required login, refresh button, minimum listening history or a network diagnosis. If the v14 UI differs, ask for platform/version and which section or named playlist they mean.

Never request passwords, cookies, tokens, callback URLs, HARs, backups or credential-bearing logs/screenshots in chat or tools. Credentials belong only in the app. Never prescribe reinstalling, clearing app data or logout as routine troubleshooting. Back up before a source-supported destructive action.
